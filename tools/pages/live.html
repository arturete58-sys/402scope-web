Live · Stellar testnet

What the contracts did, as it happened

Attesters lock a bond, write scores onchain, and agent wallets pay only the sellers those scores trust, within a budget. This page reads those events in your browser, straight from a public Stellar RPC. No server of ours sits in between.

The contracts are redeployed every day by an open workflow that runs the whole flow with real testnet payments. Testnet tokens have no value.
