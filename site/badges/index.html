Badges

Show what was measured, not what was claimed

A live badge for any endpoint in the paid panel. It shows the fault rate upper bound and the sample it rests on, and links to the full record. It updates itself every ten minutes.

Free and automatic. It cannot be bought, earned by contact with the observatory, or switched off for a bad week: it shows the measurement as it is.
